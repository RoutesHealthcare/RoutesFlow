Getting started

Drag a shape onto the page, or click one to drop it in the middle. Double-click a shape, or select it and start typing, to edit its text. Use Connect to draw lines between shapes; they stay attached when you move things.

The Text tab shows the diagram as text. Edit either side and the other follows. Save keeps the text as a .flow file; Export makes Visio, Mermaid, PlantUML, DOT or draw.io.
