Getting started

Drag a shape onto the page, or click one to drop it in the middle. To edit a shape's text, click it once to select it, then click it again, press Enter or just start typing (double-click works too). To select several shapes, drag a box around them on an empty part of the page, or Shift+click each one. Use Connect to draw lines between shapes; they stay attached when you move things.

The Text tab shows the diagram as text. Edit either side and the other follows. Save keeps the text as a .flow file. Import brings in Visio drawings and Mermaid, PlantUML, DOT or draw.io; Export makes any of them.
